## What to know before combining Kohl’s Cash with a coupon

Kohl’s Cash is not the same thing as a percentage-off promo code. It is a store-issued promotional credit that can be earned during specified periods and redeemed during a later window.

Kohl’s currently advertises an earning structure of **$10 Kohl’s Cash for every $50 spent** during the listed promotional period, with separate dates for redemption. The qualifying purchase amount is calculated after applicable coupons and before tax. Sephora at Kohl’s merchandise and gift cards are excluded from the earning calculation.

This means the following order structure can matter:

- You buy $60 of eligible merchandise.
- A percentage coupon reduces the qualifying subtotal.
- Kohl’s calculates Kohl’s Cash from the adjusted merchandise amount.
- Tax and shipping are handled separately.
- The Kohl’s Cash may become available during a later redemption period rather than immediately.

A common mistake is assuming that spending $50 before a coupon always guarantees $10 in Kohl’s Cash. If a coupon lowers the qualifying merchandise subtotal below the required threshold, the result may be different.

Kohl’s says dollar-off discounts, including Kohl’s Cash, are generally deducted before a percentage-off coupon. That can make the final result different from simply multiplying the original subtotal by the advertised percentage.

## Why a Kohl’s coupon code may not work

When a Kohl’s promo code fails, the reason is usually one of the following:

### The offer has expired

Kohl’s coupons typically become valid at 12:00 a.m. Central Time on the first day of the offer and expire at 11:59 p.m. Central Time on the final day unless the offer says otherwise. A code can therefore stop working at a specific time even if it appeared in a search result earlier that day.

### The cart contains excluded items

One excluded item may not always invalidate the entire order, but it can prevent the discount from applying to that item or reduce the qualifying subtotal. Remove excluded items temporarily and test the coupon again.

### The minimum spend is calculated differently

A $50 offer may require $50 in qualifying merchandise before tax and shipping. Gift cards, excluded brands, and other non-qualifying products may not count toward the threshold.

### The code is tied to a customer group

Some offers are available only to Kohl’s Rewards members, Kohl’s Card customers, mobile users, or people who received a specific email or mailed coupon. The same code may not work on every account.

### Another discount conflicts with it

Kohl’s allows some coupon combinations but not every combination. A sitewide percentage coupon, a department-specific code, and Kohl’s Cash are handled differently. If several codes are entered, the system may reject one or apply only the valid combination.

### The code was not featured on ShopBack

This issue matters when cashback is part of your plan. ShopBack’s Kohl’s terms specifically exclude purchases made with vouchers or promo codes that are not featured on its platform.
